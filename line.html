Complete the Line

Trace the pattern and continue it to the edge. Strengthens pencil control for the shapes used in handwriting.

Tips for grown-ups

  • Trace first

    Go over the dashed part slowly before continuing alone — speed comes later.

  • Big before small

    Large patterns are easier for small hands; move to smaller ones as control improves.

  • Check the grip

    Chunky crayons or short pencils help small hands find a comfortable hold.
